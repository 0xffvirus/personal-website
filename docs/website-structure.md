# Portfolio Website Structure

## Summary

Build a single-page portfolio following `guide.md` and `docs/design-docs.md`.

**Section order:** Header → Hero → Selected Projects → About Me & Skills → Contact → Footer.

## Sections and rationale

- **Header:** Name and navigation links to Projects, About, and Contact. Use compact floating navigation for easy access.
- **Hero:** Name, role, short introduction, primary “View my work” action, and secondary “Contact me” link. Establish identity and lead visitors toward project evidence.
- **Selected Projects:** At least two projects with images, titles, descriptions, technologies, and real repository/demo links where available. Demonstrate abilities before presenting the longer biography.
- **About Me & Skills:** A 2–3 sentence biography, education, relevant skills, and optional portrait. Add personal context without creating unnecessary sections.
- **Contact:** Invitation, direct email link, and Name, Email, Message form. Use JavaScript validation; clearly identify the frontend-only demo and never claim delivery.
- **Footer:** Name, copyright, and available GitHub/LinkedIn links. Provide a simple closing.

## Design and verification

Use the reference’s neutral palette, expressive typography, generous spacing, rounded images, and pill buttons. Projects use two columns on desktop and one on mobile.

Check responsive layouts, keyboard navigation, working links, readable contrast, and form validation.

## Assumptions

The initial audience is internship recruiters and potential collaborators. Keep services, testimonials, FAQs, and a blog out of the first version. No backend is required.
